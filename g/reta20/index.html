PROFESSIONAL HANDLING REFERENCE

RETATRUTIDE RECONSTITUTION

One 20 mg vial at a time

INVESTIGATIONAL PRODUCT - NOT A SELF-INJECTION OR DOSING GUIDE

WHAT IS IN THE KIT

  • 2 × 20 mg lyophilised Retatrutide vials
  • 1 × 3 mL bacteriostatic (BAC) water
  • 5 × sterile U-100 syringes and alcohol swabs
RECONSTITUTE ONE 20 mg VIAL WITH 1.25 mL BAC WATER
1

PREPARE ASEPTICALLY

Wash hands and use a clean surface. Wipe both vial stoppers with an alcohol swab and allow them to dry completely.

2

DRAW 100 UNITS (1.00 mL)

Using a new sterile U-100 syringe, draw BAC water to the 100-unit mark.

3

ADD SLOWLY DOWN THE VIAL WALL

Tilt the Retatrutide vial slightly and slowly direct the BAC water onto the inner glass wall - not forcefully onto the powder.

4

ADD THE FINAL 25 UNITS (0.25 mL)

Using a second new sterile syringe, draw 25 units and add it slowly in the same way. Total BAC water added: 1.25 mL.

5

GENTLY SWIVEL - NEVER SHAKE

Slowly roll or swivel until fully dissolved. Do not shake. Do not use if unexpectedly cloudy, discoloured, or containing particles.

FINAL CONCENTRATION
20 mg / 1.25 mL = 16 mg/mL U-100 REFERENCE
100 units = 1 mL

HANDLING & STORAGE

  • Label the vial with the reconstitution date and time.
  • Use a new sterile syringe and needle for every puncture.
  • Keep refrigerated at 2-8 °C only when validated by the product label or a qualified professional. Do not freeze.
  • Store unopened components exactly as labelled; protect from heat, light, and contamination.
  • Discard if damage, contamination, or a storage excursion is suspected. Follow the product-specific beyond-use date.
