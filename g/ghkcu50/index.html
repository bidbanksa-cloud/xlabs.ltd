X-LABS GHK-CU 50 MG VIALS RECONSTITUTION & HANDLING GUIDE

Simple instructions for preparing one 50 mg vial using bacteriostatic water.

1. Package contents

  • 2 × 50 mg GHK-Cu vials
    (lyophilised peptide powder)
  • 2 × 3 mL bacteriostatic water
  • 5 × U-100 insulin syringes
  • Alcohol swabs

2. Before you start

  • Wash and dry your hands thoroughly.
  • Work on a clean surface.
  • Use a new sterile syringe.
  • Do not use if seals are damaged.

4. Storage

  • Store the reconstituted vial in a refrigerator at 2–8 °C.
  • Keep the unreconstituted second vial and remaining bacteriostatic water in a safe, dry place until needed.
  • Protect from heat, light and contamination.
❄

5. Important notes

  • Use only as directed by your medical practitioner.
  • Do not shake the vial – peptide compounds can be sensitive.
  • Administration amount and schedule must be determined by a qualified practitioner.
  • Keep all components sterile during handling.

6. Disclaimer

For research / specialist use only. This guide provides general handling and reconstitution information only and does not replace medical advice. Use is subject to practitioner guidance and applicable law.
